D-MorphNet — Face Morph Detector

EfficientNet-B6 deep features + SVM (RBF). Upload a face photo and the model decides whether it is a real photograph or a morph of two people.

P(morph)
Research demo trained on a small landmark-morph dataset (85% accuracy / 0.935 AUC on identity-disjoint test data) — expect mistakes on unusual photos, and do not use for any real identity-verification decision.
